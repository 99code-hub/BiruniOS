# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in BiruniOS a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent while addressing harmful impact.
- Welcome different backgrounds, experience levels, and viewpoints.
- Focus criticism on ideas and code, not people.
- Accept responsibility and learn from mistakes.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, threats, doxxing, sexualized attention, and sustained disruptive behavior are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, reject contributions, or restrict participation for behavior that violates this code. Reports should be sent privately to the maintainers through the repository's security/private contact route.
